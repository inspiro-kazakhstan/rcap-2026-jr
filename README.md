  <h4>Summary:</h4>
  Our performance is a synchronized show of robots, where each one of eight moves to their respective coordinates. A camera towered above the stadium constantly monitors their positions by estimating the coordinates of ArUco markers on top of each robot. After processing them through a computer, they get sent to the corresponding LEGO SPIKE Prime hub. Using the Pythagorean theorem, the hub's inner code calculates the distance between its current and target positions. Then, it powers up its motors and drives there.
  We also connected a neon light strip to one of the ports, marking it as a DC motor. Each shape turns on a different color: sometimes static, sometimes pulsing. The computer also plays music throughout the show.
  Another feature is voice recognition: when someone says "RoboCup 2026 is open!", the robots start moving.
